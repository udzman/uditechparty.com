So… what's a Tech Party?

No recipe. Made with you.

There's no off-the-shelf answer to how your business should run, so I don't sell you one. We sit down, work out what's actually slowing you down, and build a plan that fits the business you've become — not a template, not whatever a vendor happened to be selling.

You're not a customer getting handed a product. You're a collaborator — and you walk away with something that's genuinely yours.
Sound familiar?

It's time to build a workflow.

A spreadsheet here, a shared drive there, a process that lived in one person's head. It worked — better than anyone expected.

Now there are more people and more moving parts, and the old way is quietly costing you. Nothing's broken, exactly. It just doesn't keep up anymore. Every new hire makes it a little more fragile. Every workaround takes a little more out of you.

You know it's time to do something about your tech — you're just not sure what, and you don't want to pour time, money, and energy into the wrong move.
