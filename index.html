What's a Tech Party?

No recipe. Made with you.

There's no off-the-shelf answer to how a business should run, so I don't start with one. I work out what's actually slowing things down and shape a plan that fits the business you've become — not a template, not whatever a vendor happened to be selling.

Plain language, no jargon, and something that's genuinely yours at the end of it — you're a collaborator, not a customer handed a product.
