how i work

Most technology problems in operations are workflow problems wearing a technology costume. So before I recommend anything, I spend time watching how your team works now, where the handoffs are and where things get stuck. You can't automate a workflow you don't understand as a human process first.

From there we decide together what to build, what to buy and what to leave alone. I don't resell software, so the advice isn't tied to a vendor, and whatever we build is documented and stays yours.

where i help

A spreadsheet here, a shared drive there, a process that lives in one person's head. It worked for a long time. Then the team grew, and every new hire made it a little more fragile. The work I usually take on:

  • Salesforce architecture, cleanup and custom builds
  • Automating administrative workflows end to end, in Flow, Apex, Apps Script and Python
  • Practical AI adoption, starting from the workflow rather than the tool
  • Google Workspace, devices and the IT basics a small team needs
about
